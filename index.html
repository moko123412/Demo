🦁 Menagerie

Build the zoo you always dreamed of.

  1. 🚧 Fences: tap or click tile edges to place fence pieces, or drag to draw straight runs (Rectangle mode fences a whole area). A closed fence makes an exhibit.
  2. ⛰️ Terrain and 🌳 Plants: shape the habitat. Each animal has favourites.
  3. 🦁 Animals: adopt animals and click inside the exhibit to release them.
  4. 🧢 Staff: hire zookeepers to feed animals and clean exhibits.
  5. 🍔 Guests: add food, drinks, restrooms, benches and trash cans along the paths.
  6. Click an animal, guest or exhibit to see what they think. Happy animals bring happy guests, and happy guests bring money.

Drag, WASD or push the mouse to the screen edge to scroll. Wheel, pinch or ＋/－ to zoom. Right-click or Esc cancels a tool, Ctrl+Z undoes. Tool keys: Q select, T terrain, P paths, F fences, N animals, L plants, E exhibit items, B food, R rides, V decor, H staff, X bulldoze, M minimap. Space pauses, G shows the grid.
