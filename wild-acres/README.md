# Wild Acres

An original zoo-management game that runs in the browser. Open `index.html`; nothing to install.

- **Build:** lay footpaths and drag out savanna, forest, tundra or wetland habitats.
- **Animals:** 17 species. Each one wants the right biome, enough space, company (or solitude), food, a clean enclosure and enrichment decor. Predators frighten prey kept in the same habitat. Happy pairs have babies.
- **Guests:** they walk the paths, look at habitats, and buy food, drinks and souvenirs. They also get tired, need restrooms and drop litter.
- **Staff:** zookeepers feed and clean habitats; janitors sweep the paths.
- **Economy:** set the ticket price, pay wages and upkeep, and earn star ratings that unlock rarer species. Complete park goals for cash bonuses.

Controls: drag to pan, scroll or use +/− to zoom, Space to pause, 1/2/3 to change speed, Esc to cancel a tool. Progress saves in your browser at the end of each day.

`game.html` is the source. `build.sh` wraps it into the standalone `index.html`.
